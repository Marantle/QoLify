# SoundScaper - Volume Profiles for Dungeon, Raid, Mythic+ and Boss Fights

Different places deserve different sound. Full music out in the world, music down low once
you step into your house, effects-only in a key, everything muted except Master the moment a
raid boss is pulled. SoundScaper switches your volume settings automatically when you zone in
or out, so you stop digging through the sound options between activities.

## What it does

Six sections, each with its own volume slider and mute checkbox per audio channel (Master,
Effects, Music, Ambience, Dialog), plus the sound channel count, so a raid can run more
simultaneous sounds than a quiet evening of questing:

- **Outdoors** - open world, cities, battlegrounds, arenas
- **House** - indoors in a player house, yours or someone else's. The yard still counts as
  Outdoors
- **Dungeon** - any 5-player dungeon
- **Mythic+** - keystone runs, detected from the moment you zone in
- **Raid** - inside a raid instance, between pulls
- **Raid Boss** - switches the instant a boss encounter starts, back when it ends

House, Mythic+ and Raid Boss follow Outdoors, Dungeon and Raid until you tick "Use separate
settings here" on their tab, so you can configure just three sections and be done.

Unlike addons with a single "instanced content" profile, every one of these is its own section.
A heroic dungeon, a +15 and a mythic raid boss can all sound different.

The sync goes both ways. Adjust a volume in the game's own audio options and the section you
are currently in picks up the change, so you can keep using the Blizzard panel and SoundScaper
will not fight it.

## Usage

Left-click the minimap button or type `/ss` to open the window. Right-click the minimap button
toggles the addon on and off. `/ss help` lists the slash commands.

## Part of QoLify

SoundScaper also ships as a module of
[QoLify](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/qolify), my quality of life suite. Install
whichever you prefer, your settings follow you between them. If both are installed, the
standalone runs and the module stands by.
