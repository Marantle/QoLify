# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- The bar now hides inside dungeons, raids and delves and comes back when
  you leave. A 12.1 hotfix stopped addons from reading the minimap compass
  in instances, which was the only thing that turned the bar in there.
- The instance code is gone with it. That's the pieced wheel, the minimap
  rotation it borrowed and the ring art. If a crash on 0.1.0 left your
  minimap rotation switched on, it goes back off at login.

## 0.1.0
- First build. A compass bar across the screen: the direction letters
  slide past a marker as you turn, with a mark under each letter in a color
  of your choosing. Out in the world it carries places from the map, each
  kind with its own switch and a range in yards: tracked quests, world
  quests, quests on offer, your map pin, group members, target and focus,
  entrances, flight points, graveyards, points of interest, rares and
  treasures. A gear on the bar flips them without opening the settings.
