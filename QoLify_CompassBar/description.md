# CompassBar

A compass bar across the top of the screen, the kind you know from Skyrim. The
direction letters slide past a marker as you turn, so you always know which way
you face without glancing at the minimap.

Out in the world the bar also carries places from the map, each with a little
icon and, if you like, its distance in yards: the quest you are tracking and
the objectives of your other tracked quests, world quests, quest givers, your
map pin, the members of your group in their class colors, your target and
focus, dungeon and raid entrances, flight points, graveyards, points of
interest, rares and treasures. Every kind has its own switch and a range, so
you can keep rares on the bar only once they are within a few hundred yards,
say. A gear on the bar's corner flips the switches without opening the
settings.

Inside dungeons, raids and delves the game keeps your facing from addons, so
the bar hides there and comes back as soon as you leave.

Type `/cbar` for the settings, `/cbar on` and `/cbar off` to show and hide it,
`/cbar lock` to lock it in place.

CompassBar is also a module of the QoLify suite. If you run both, the module
stands by while this addon is enabled and picks up your settings once it is
removed.
